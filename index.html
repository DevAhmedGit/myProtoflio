إزّاي

Ezzay

ezzay = "how" in Egyptian Arabic

An app that teaches foreigners Egyptian Arabic, the dialect people actually speak, not Modern Standard Arabic.

What is built

  • Real-life lessons by situation (café, food, taxi, shopping) with search, category filters and progress bars
  • A roleplay screen where a waiter greets you and you answer by voice or by typing, with hints
  • Arabic Basics: all 28 letters, each with its sound and an Egyptian Arabic example word
  • Phrase detail screens with audio and usage notes, plus a saved phrases list
  • A profile with day streak, weekly goal and settings, in light and dark themes

How it is built

Screens are made from reusable widgets. They read from typed models in lib/models and local sample data in lib/data, linked together by model IDs.

Stack

FlutterDartReusable widgetsModel-driven dataLight and dark themesFirebase Auth (next)

Next: Firebase email and password sign-in (the login and sign-up screens are built) and saved progress on the device.
